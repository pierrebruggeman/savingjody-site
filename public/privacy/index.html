Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Saving Jody is a free mobile game. This policy explains what information is collected when you play it, who collects it and the choices you have. In short: the game itself collects no personal information about you. The only data collected is by Google AdMob, the service that provides the optional rewarded ads.

What the game stores

Your progress (levels completed, bananas, rescued animals, outfits and settings) is saved only on your device. There are no accounts, no logins and no chat. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Saving Jody shows ads only when you choose to watch one, for example to get a second chance or extra bananas. These rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system and language;
  • information about ad interactions, such as ads shown and tapped;
  • diagnostic data such as crashes and performance of the ad service.

If you do not allow tracking, ads are still shown but are not personalized using your advertising identifier. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you.

Your choices

  • Turn off tracking at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalization in iOS Settings.
  • Simply never tap an ad button: the whole game can be played without watching any ads.
  • Delete the game to remove all of its saved data from your device.

Playtester sign-up (website)

If you sign up as a playtester on savingjody.com, we store the email address, the optional first name and the device type you enter, in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). We use them only to send you playtest invitations and news about the playtest. Invitations are sent through Apple TestFlight (iPhone) or Firebase App Distribution (Android), which then also process your email address. We never sell your details or use them for advertising. To be removed from the list, email support@savingjody.com and we will delete your details.

Children

Saving Jody is suitable for all ages, and ads are limited to content rated for general audiences. The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent and believe your child's information was collected, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are handled by Google; you can also contact us and we will help you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@savingjody.com
