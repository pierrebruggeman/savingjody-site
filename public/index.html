Swing with just two thumbs!

Kaia swings from vine to vine across 12 painted worlds. She is on a mission to save Jody, a little monkey grabbed by the Storm Toucan. Every cage she opens sets another animal friend free.

Swing, jump, fly

Hold to grab a vine, let go to jump. Fly over waterfalls, through caves and above the clouds. Easy to learn, fun to master.

12 painted worlds

Explore a turquoise jungle, a sunset jungle, a sparkly crystal cave and the land above the clouds. Every world has its own big boss.

The adventure keeps going

Beat a boss and Jody is free… until the storm comes back! New worlds arrive in free updates.
