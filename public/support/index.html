Support

Found a bug, stuck on a level or have an idea? Email support@savingjody.com and include your iPhone model and the stage you were playing (for example 3-4). Replies usually come within a few days.

Common questions

Is Saving Jody really free?
Yes. Every world and every stage can be played without paying and without watching ads. Ads only appear when you tap a button to watch one for a reward.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress.

The rewarded ad button disappeared.
It only shows when an ad is ready and there is a daily limit. Check your internet connection and try again a little later.

How do I turn off ad tracking?
Go to iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Ads will still work but won't be personalized. See the Privacy Policy for details.

When do new worlds come out?
New worlds arrive in free updates. Keep the game updated to follow Jody.
